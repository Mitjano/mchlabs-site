VOLTA — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 July 2026

Short version: VOLTA collects nothing. The game has no internet permission, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. Everything you do stays on your device.

Who we are

VOLTA is a puzzle game published by MCH Labs, a brand of juvestore.pl Michał Chmielarz, a sole proprietorship registered in Poland. Questions about this policy: michalchmielarz00@gmail.com.

What data we collect

None. We do not collect, transmit, store or share any personal data. VOLTA is not granted the Android INTERNET permission, which means the application is technically incapable of sending information anywhere.

Specifically, VOLTA does not use:

  • user accounts, sign-in or email addresses;
  • analytics, telemetry or crash reporting services;
  • advertising networks, advertising identifiers or tracking;
  • cloud saves or any server of ours;
  • location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

What is stored on your device

Your progress is saved locally on your phone using Android's standard application storage: completed levels, stars, collected Sparks, purchased boosters, daily quest streaks, and your sound and language preferences.

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling VOLTA deletes it permanently — we hold no copy and therefore cannot restore it.

Notifications

VOLTA may ask permission to send you notifications, used solely for local reminders about the daily puzzle and your streak. These are generated on your device by the game itself; no message is sent from any server and no data is transmitted to us. You can decline the permission or disable notifications in the game's settings or in Android system settings at any time.

Children

VOLTA is suitable for a general audience. Because the game collects no data whatsoever, it collects no data from children either.

Distribution

VOLTA is distributed through Google Play. Google processes your download and any store interactions under its own terms — see the Google Privacy Policy. That processing is outside our control and independent of the game itself.

Your rights

Rights such as access, correction or erasure under the GDPR, CCPA or similar laws apply to personal data held by us. As we hold none, there is nothing for us to disclose or delete. You remain in full control of the local save data on your own device.

Changes

If a future version of VOLTA introduces features that process data — for example an online leaderboard — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described in the Google Play listing.
