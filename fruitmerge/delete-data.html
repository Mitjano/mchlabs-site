Delete your data — FRUITMERGE

Last updated: 12 October 2026 · App: com.mchlabs.fruitmerge · Developer: MCH Labs · Contact: michalchmielarz00@gmail.com

FRUITMERGE is a fruit‑merge puzzle game published by MCH Labs. The first time the game starts with an internet connection, it creates an anonymous guest account (no e‑mail, no password). This page explains how to delete that account and the data tied to it.

1. Delete your data in the game

  1. Open FRUITMERGE and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete data.
  3. Confirm with Delete.

The game needs an internet connection to delete the cloud data. If the connection drops, the game tells you and nothing is erased, so you can try again.

2. What is deleted

  • Your guest account in Google Firebase Authentication (an anonymous ID).
  • Your cloud save: coins, scores, Crew, Journey, boosters, achievements, owned skins and other game progress.
  • Your entry on the online leaderboard (display name and best score).
  • Gameplay events (usage statistics) linked to your account ID.
  • Game data stored on your phone: after deletion the game starts over as for a new player. Only the language setting is kept.

Nothing is kept after deletion, except where the law requires us to keep it. Google may keep encrypted backups of its services for a limited time before they are overwritten; we do not restore data from them.

3. Delete your data by e‑mail

If you can no longer open the game (for example you uninstalled it), write to michalchmielarz00@gmail.com with the subject “FRUITMERGE data deletion”. Include your display name from the leaderboard and, if you can, roughly when you last played. We may ask a question to make sure the data is yours.

4. How long it takes

In the game, deletion happens immediately. Requests sent by e‑mail are completed within 30 days, and we confirm by e‑mail when it is done.

5. More information

See the FRUITMERGE Privacy Policy for what data the game processes and why.
