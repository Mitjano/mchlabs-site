Privacy Policy — FRUITMERGE

Last updated: 11 October 2026 · App: com.mchlabs.fruitmerge · Developer: MCH Labs · Contact: michalchmielarz00@gmail.com

1. Who we are

FRUITMERGE is a fruit‑merge puzzle game published by MCH Labs ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the game processes, why, and your choices. Questions: michalchmielarz00@gmail.com.

2. Data we process

a) Data stored only on your device. Your game progress (coins, scores, Crew collection, Journey progress, settings) is kept on your phone. If you choose online features, your sign‑in token is stored there too.

b) A pseudonymous guest account (optional). Only when you tap “Play as guest” do we create an anonymous account via Google Firebase Authentication, identified by a random ID (“UID”). It is not linked to your real identity and needs no personal details. No email address is collected.

c) Cloud backup (optional). With a guest account, a backup copy of part of your progress is stored in Google Cloud Firestore tied to your UID: coins, best score, player XP, Crew count, Journey progress and your display name. This is a backup only; the game does not offer cross‑device play.

d) Leaderboard entry (optional). With a guest account, your display name, best score and the time it was set may appear on the online leaderboard. The game assigns a random display name (e.g. “JuicyMelon42”). Do not use your real name or personal information.

We do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, or other sensitive personal data. The game shows no ads.

3. How we use data

To keep your progress; to back it up if you use a guest account; to operate the leaderboard; to prevent abuse and cheating. We do not sell your data.

4. Third‑party services

Online features use Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore), which processes data as our service provider. See Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase data handling.

5. Deleting your data

In the game: Settings → Delete data. This removes your local progress, your cloud backup, your leaderboard entry and your guest account. If anything cannot be deleted in the app (for example you no longer have the device), email michalchmielarz00@gmail.com and we will delete it within 30 days.

We keep cloud data only while your guest account exists.

6. Your choices and rights

You can play fully offline without any account. Depending on where you live (e.g. EU/EEA under GDPR, California under CCPA) you may have additional rights, such as access to your data; contact us to exercise them.

7. Children

FRUITMERGE is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children; if you believe a child provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored on Google's infrastructure with access restricted by security rules — each player can only read/write their own save.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date will change. Material changes will be reflected in the app or store listing.
