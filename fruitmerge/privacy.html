Privacy Policy — FRUITMERGE

Last updated: 12 October 2026 · App: com.mchlabs.fruitmerge · Developer: MCH Labs · Contact: michalchmielarz00@gmail.com

1. Who we are

FRUITMERGE is a fruit‑merge puzzle game published by MCH Labs ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the game processes, why, and your choices. Questions: michalchmielarz00@gmail.com.

2. Data we process

a) Data on your device. Your game progress (coins, scores, Crew collection, Journey progress, boosters, achievements, skins) and settings are kept on your phone, together with the sign‑in token of your guest account.

b) A pseudonymous guest account. The first time the game starts with an internet connection, it creates an anonymous account via Google Firebase Authentication, identified by a random ID (“UID”). It is not linked to your real identity and needs no personal details. No email address is collected. Without an internet connection the game works offline.

c) Cloud save. A copy of your game progress (coins, best scores, player XP, Crew, Journey, boosters, achievements, owned skins, season pass progress, game statistics, your install date and display name) is stored in Google Cloud Firestore tied to your UID, so it can be restored if you reinstall the game. Device settings are not included.

d) Leaderboard entry. Your display name, best score and the time it was set appear on the online leaderboard, visible to other players. The game assigns a random display name (e.g. “JuicyMelon42”); it is not your real name.

e) Gameplay events. To fix bugs and balance difficulty, the game sends simple gameplay events (for example game started or finished with its score, tutorial steps, boosters and rewards used, sessions started) with the app version, a timestamp, days since install and your UID to our Firestore database. We do not use Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics or advertising identifiers.

f) Sharing. If you tap “Share”, the game creates an image of your score and opens Android’s share sheet. It goes only to the app you choose; we do not receive it.

g) Notifications. If you allow notifications, reminders are scheduled on your device. No push token is created and nothing is sent to us. You can turn them off in Settings.

We do not collect advertising identifiers, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, or other sensitive personal data. The game shows no ads and has no in‑app purchases for real money.

3. How we use data

To keep and restore your progress; to operate the leaderboard; to keep the game working, find bugs and balance difficulty; to prevent abuse and cheating. We do not sell your data. The legal basis (EU/EEA) is the performance of the game service you requested and our legitimate interest in keeping it working and fair.

4. Third‑party services

The game uses Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore), which processes data as our service provider. See Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase data handling. The game is built with the Unity engine, which may send basic technical information about your device (for example model and operating system) to Unity Technologies; see Unity's Privacy Policy.

5. Deleting your data

In the game: Settings → Delete data. This removes your cloud save, your leaderboard entry, your gameplay events, your guest account and your progress on this device. If anything cannot be deleted in the app (for example you no longer have the device), email michalchmielarz00@gmail.com with your display name and we will delete it within 30 days.

Step‑by‑step instructions: How to delete your FRUITMERGE data.

We keep cloud data only while your guest account exists.

6. Your choices and rights

You can play offline; no email is ever required; you can delete your data at any time. Depending on where you live (e.g. EU/EEA under GDPR, California under CCPA) you may have additional rights, such as access to your data or objecting to processing; contact us to exercise them.

7. Children

FRUITMERGE is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children; if you believe a child provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored on Google's infrastructure with access restricted by security rules — each player can only read/write their own save.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date will change. Material changes will be reflected in the app or store listing.
