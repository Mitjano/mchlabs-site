Privacy Policy — LUMEN

Last updated: 12 October 2026 · App: com.mchlabs.lumen · Developer: MCH Labs · Contact: michalchmielarz00@gmail.com

1. Who we are

LUMEN is a match‑3 puzzle game published by MCH Labs ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the game processes, why, and your choices. Questions: michalchmielarz00@gmail.com.

2. Data we process

a) A pseudonymous account identifier. On first launch we create an anonymous account via Google Firebase Authentication, identified by a random ID (“UID”). It is not linked to your real identity and needs no personal details.

b) Your game progress (cloud save). To prevent progress loss and let you continue on another device, we store gameplay data in Google Cloud Firestore tied to your UID: levels completed, stars, coins, streaks, boosters, owned cosmetics (skins, frames, avatars), your display name and any team code.

c) Your display name (“nick”). The game assigns a random display name (e.g. “SzybkiTygrys7297”); you can change it in Settings. Names are checked by a profanity filter. Do not use your real name or personal information.

d) Optional email address. Linking an email and password is optional; if you do, your email is stored by Firebase Authentication so you can restore progress on another device. You can play the whole game without an email.

e) Public leaderboard and team data. Your display name and star count appear on the global leaderboard and weekly tournament. Team names are public in the team league. In a team, your display name and contribution are visible to its members, and a team gift shows the sender’s display name. There is no chat in the game.

f) Gameplay events. To fix bugs and balance difficulty, the game sends simple gameplay events (for example level started or finished, tutorial steps) with the app version, a timestamp and your UID to our Firestore database. We do not use Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics or advertising identifiers, and the game shows no ads.

g) Remote settings. To fetch balance settings, the game sends a random installation identifier to Firebase Remote Config.

h) Error reports. If the game hits a software error, it sends a short error report (error message and code location, app version, device model and Android version, time and your UID) to our Firestore database so we can fix it. Reports are limited per session and contain no game content beyond that.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, or other sensitive personal data.

3. How we use data

To save and restore progress; to operate the leaderboard, tournament, teams and league; to keep the game working and balance difficulty; to prevent abuse and cheating. We do not sell your data.

4. Third‑party services

LUMEN uses Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Remote Config), which processes data as our service provider. See Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase data handling. The game is built with the Unity engine, which may send basic technical information about your device (for example model and operating system) to Unity Technologies; see Unity's Privacy Policy.

5. Deleting your account and data

In the game: Account → Delete account. This removes your cloud save, profile, leaderboard and tournament entries, team membership, gameplay events, error reports sent from this device and your account, and resets the game on your device. Step‑by‑step instructions: How to delete your LUMEN account.

If you cannot use the app, email michalchmielarz00@gmail.com from the address linked to your account (or include your display name) and we will delete your data within 30 days. We keep data only while your account exists.

6. Your choices and rights

Play fully anonymously (email never required); change your public display name in Settings; delete your account at any time. Depending on where you live (e.g. EU/EEA under GDPR, California under CCPA) you may have additional rights, such as access to your data; contact us to exercise them.

7. Children

LUMEN is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children; if you believe a child provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored on Google's infrastructure with access restricted by security rules — each player can only read/write their own save.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date will change. Material changes will be reflected in the app or store listing.
