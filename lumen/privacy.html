Privacy Policy — LUMEN

Last updated: 4 July 2026 · App: com.mchlabs.lumen · Developer: MCH Labs · Contact: michalchmielarz00@gmail.com

1. Who we are

LUMEN is a match‑3 puzzle game published by MCH Labs ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the game processes, why, and your choices. Questions: michalchmielarz00@gmail.com.

2. Data we process

a) A pseudonymous account identifier. On first launch we create an anonymous account via Google Firebase Authentication, identified by a random ID ("UID"). It is not linked to your real identity and needs no personal details.

b) Your game progress (cloud save). To prevent progress loss and enable cross‑device play, we store gameplay data in Google Cloud Firestore tied to your UID: levels completed, stars, coins, streaks, season‑pass progress, your display name, and any team code.

c) Your display name ("nick"). The game assigns a random display name (e.g. "SzybkiTygrys7297"); you can change it in Settings. This name is shown publicly on the global leaderboard and to members of any team you join. Do not use your real name or personal information as your display name.

d) Optional email address. Linking an email + password is optional; if you do, your email is stored by Firebase Authentication to restore progress on another device. You can play the whole game without an email.

e) Leaderboard and team data. Your display name and total star count are published to a global leaderboard visible to other players. In a team, your display name and star contribution are visible to that team's members.

f) Diagnostic and usage data. We may collect anonymous, aggregated gameplay and diagnostic data (e.g. levels started/finished, crashes, approximate device/region) via Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to fix bugs and balance difficulty. This is tied to a pseudonymous identifier, not your name.

We do not knowingly collect precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, or other sensitive personal data.

3. How we use data

To save and restore progress; to operate the leaderboard, teams, and season pass; to keep the game working, fix crashes, and balance difficulty; to prevent abuse and cheating. We do not sell your data.

4. Third‑party services

LUMEN uses Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics), which processes data as our service provider. See Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase data handling.

5. Data retention

We keep your cloud save and account data while your account exists. Request deletion any time by emailing michalchmielarz00@gmail.com; we will delete it within 30 days.

6. Your choices and rights

Play fully anonymously (email never required); change or randomise your public display name in Settings; request access to or deletion of your data. Depending on where you live (e.g. EU/EEA under GDPR, California under CCPA) you may have additional rights — contact us to exercise them.

7. Children

LUMEN is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children; if you believe a child provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored on Google's infrastructure with access restricted by security rules — each player can only read/write their own save.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date will change. Material changes will be reflected in the app or store listing.
