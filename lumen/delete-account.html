Delete your account — LUMEN

Last updated: 12 October 2026 · App: com.mchlabs.lumen · Developer: MCH Labs · Contact: michalchmielarz00@gmail.com

LUMEN is a match‑3 puzzle game published by MCH Labs. Every player gets an account automatically: an anonymous one on first launch, or an e‑mail account if you linked an e‑mail. This page explains how to delete that account and the data tied to it.

1. Delete your account in the game

  1. Open LUMEN and, on the main menu, tap your profile picture in the top‑right corner. The Account screen opens.
  2. Tap Delete account near the bottom of the screen.
  3. If your account has an e‑mail, type your password in the field that appears and tap Delete account again. (Forgot it? Use Forgot password on the same screen, or e‑mail us.)
  4. Confirm twice: Continue, then Delete forever.

The game needs an internet connection for this. If the connection drops, the game tells you that the account was not deleted and you can try again; nothing is left half‑deleted without a message.

2. What is deleted

  • Your account in Google Firebase Authentication (anonymous ID, and your e‑mail and password if you linked them).
  • Your cloud save: levels, stars, coins, boosters, owned skins, frames and avatars, streaks and other game progress.
  • Your shared MCH Labs profile record (display name, last game played, last seen time).
  • Your rows on the global leaderboard and in every weekly tournament.
  • Your membership in a team. If you lead a team with other members, leadership passes to the most active member; if you are the last member, the team is deleted.
  • Game data stored on your phone: after deletion the game starts over as for a new player.
  • Usage statistics (telemetry) linked to your account ID.
  • Error reports sent from this phone. Reports sent from another device can be removed on request by e‑mail.
  • Team chat messages you sent in earlier versions of the game (team chat is currently switched off).

Nothing is kept after deletion, except where the law requires us to keep it. Google may keep encrypted backups of its services for a limited time before they are overwritten; we do not restore data from them.

3. Delete your account by e‑mail

If you can no longer open the game (for example you uninstalled it), write to michalchmielarz00@gmail.com with the subject “LUMEN account deletion”. Include the e‑mail linked to your account, or your display name (nick) from the leaderboard. We may ask a question to make sure the account is yours.

4. How long it takes

In the game, deletion happens immediately. Requests sent by e‑mail are completed within 30 days, and we confirm by e‑mail when it is done.

5. More information

See the LUMEN Privacy Policy for what data the game processes and why.

Usunięcie konta (PL)

W grze: w menu głównym dotknij swojego zdjęcia profilowego w prawym górnym rogu, otwórz ekran Konto i wybierz Usuń konto. Przy koncie z e‑mailem wpisz hasło i dotknij przycisku jeszcze raz. Potwierdź dwa razy: Dalej, potem Usuń na zawsze. Potrzebny jest internet; jeśli połączenie zerwie się w trakcie, gra o tym powie i można ponowić.

Co usuwamy: konto w Firebase Authentication (z e‑mailem i hasłem, jeśli były), zapis w chmurze (postęp, monety, dopalacze, kupione skórki, ramki i awatary), profil MCH Labs, wpisy w rankingu i turniejach, członkostwo w drużynie (lider przekazuje dowództwo, ostatni członek usuwa drużynę), statystyki użycia powiązane z kontem, raporty błędów wysłane z tego telefonu (z innych urządzeń usuwamy na prośbę e‑mailem), wiadomości wysłane na czacie drużyny we wcześniejszych wersjach gry oraz zapis na telefonie.

E‑mailem: jeśli nie masz już gry, napisz na michalchmielarz00@gmail.com z tematem „LUMEN usunięcie konta” i podaj e‑mail konta albo swój nick z rankingu. Realizujemy to w ciągu 30 dni i potwierdzamy e‑mailem. W grze usunięcie jest natychmiastowe.
